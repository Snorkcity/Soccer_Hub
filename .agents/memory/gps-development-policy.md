name: Player Development methodology
description: Approved reference policy, practical defaults, retained evidence and limits on interpretation
---

The coach approved building Player Development inside GPS Insights, keeping the existing charts and reports intact. It supports the men's U23 and first grade in 2027 without applying adult-male research values to women.

**Why:** the coach accepted the recommendation after reviewing their supplied BUFC methodology; they delegated practical policy decisions.

**How to apply:** use a separate development view; preserve existing metrics, data and women's behaviour. Keep the future coaches' methodology presentation distinct from player development reports.

Personal Gref is metric-specific: official appearances of at least 60 minutes with comparable, valid measurements. Fewer than 3 qualifying values means insufficient; 3–4 uses the best 3 (provisional); 5+ uses the best 5 (established). Choose best matches independently for every metric. Short appearances stay visible. Percentages are descriptive and may exceed 100%.

**Why:** this gives usable personal references without projecting short appearances into fictional full matches or treating a multi-metric reference as one real performance.

**How to apply:** never replace insufficient references with zero. Separate current-season average/Gref, previous seasons, and comparable retained-history Gref/recorded highs. Saved feedback must preserve the reference evidence from its save time.

Recorded match roles take precedence. When unavailable, the usual role is a pragmatic, explicitly inferred default, not a confirmed historical fact. Mixed-role games can inform personal references but do not enter a single-role cohort. Observed same-role cohorts are informational, not approved First Grade standards.

**Why:** the coach recognises that ideal data collection is not always practical; changing a usual role must not overwrite a recorded match role.

**How to apply:** disclose inference/sample counts and permit coach corrections without rewriting wearable measurements. Keep 6 and 10 distinct football roles with similar physical emphasis.

No overall readiness grade, training-load prescription, activated selection/readiness label, or external acceleration/deceleration target.

**Why:** the supplied methodology calls for developmental, contextual evidence rather than pass/fail selection; the coach has not approved a club readiness population or thresholds.

**How to apply:** retain source, population, original thresholds, reference type and comparability for external research. Do not equate literature using >19 km/h with Catapult HSP >18 km/h.
