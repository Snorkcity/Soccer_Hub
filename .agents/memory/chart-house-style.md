---
name: Chart house style — average lines
description: Coach preference for reference lines on trend charts
---
Coach explicitly asked (Aug 2026): "where needed, make average lines in charts."
**Rule:** every per-round/per-match trend chart gets dashed season-average ReferenceLines,
one per side in the side's colour (C_US/C_THEM), noted in the CardDescription with the value.
**Why:** he reads charts as "this game vs our normal"; the average is the anchor.
**How to apply:** any new trend chart in Veo/GPS/season areas; percent-style charts also keep
their fixed midline (e.g. 50% field tilt) alongside the average.
